# Credits

Every 3D model in this project is free to redistribute in a public repository. No paid Sketchfab, Fab, TurboSquid, Synty, or Mixamo files are included.

## Characters

The bots are original three.js extrusions of the `iconShape` marks in `data/bots.json`. No downloaded character model is used. Hover, walk, work, and sleep are procedural.

## Furniture

Kenney Furniture Kit 1.0. CC0 1.0 Universal.

- https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- Mirror used for the original zip: https://opengameart.org/content/furniture-kit
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Desks, office chairs, monitors, keyboards, laptops, sofas, lounge chairs, tables, bookcases, books, televisions, speakers, rugs, lamps, plants, and the kitchen appliances (fridge, stove, sink, cabinets, coffee machine) come from this pack. FBX files were converted to GLB for the web. Kenney asks for a credit when you can, and it is not required by the license.

## Original scene work

Walls, floors, the projector body, the projection cone, screen graphics, poster art, name labels, and the UI are original and not taken from those packs. Poster titles are invented. No film stills or real movie posters are used.
